# Church service — input list and checks

Fill this in for your room. Keep it generic enough to share; leave volunteer phone numbers and personal notes in a private list.

## Before people arrive

- [ ] Confirm service order and any special music or playback
- [ ] Power on mixer, speakers, and wireless; listen for buzz or a bad channel
- [ ] Walk the wireless and put fresh batteries in packs that will be used
- [ ] Set a conservative starting gain on spoken-word mics
- [ ] Check playback from the computer or media player through the mains and any stream mix
- [ ] Confirm the stream or record feed, if you have one, is  receiving audio
