# Church service — input list and checks

Church Service will be catered to my Church, Bear Creek Community Church. We Utilize an Allen & Heath SQ 5 with an Allen $ Heath AB168 extension box behind our stage which has 2 8 input 4 return stage extension boxes out of that to get closer to the bands members locations. We have 4 single channel DI Boxes and 2 Dual Channel DI Boxes for instruments, 6 or so Microphones, an assorted collection of XLR cables, ranging from 1 foot whites to 6 foot purples and reds. The 6ft cables are the same but the colors indicate where the wraps are attached. Purples have the wrap near the male "output" side for microphones and instruments, while the reds have the wrap near the female "input" side for the in-ear returns and amped speaker returns. We have 8 hip return amps and 2 ME 500 personal returns with headphones that people can use due to the stage is behind our speakers. We have 2 wireless handheld microphones and 1 over the ear "Britney Spears" Mic. We run 2 PQ 5 Speakers hanging from the center of our peaked ceiling. 



## Saturday Setup
Setup is around noon the day before, check on Planning Center for the band setup by the worship leader
- [ ] Gather needed cables, mics, di boxes, returns, etc, Turn on Board
- [ ] Setup on stage for that worship leaders typical layout,
- [ ] Main Vocalist is near the drums on stage left, backup vocalists and band is on the other side of, or behind the standing location for the Pastor/Speaker box
- [ ] Align everything with the setup for inputs and outputs. Layer E is our main operating layer.
- [ ] Pull Libraries for the band
- [ ] Use the SQ 5's I/O pane for order of outputs config against I/O order below
- [ ] Create ME 500 sticky-note for the output layout based on I/O config
- [ ] Save the setup to the Services Scene
- [ ] Shutdown Board, lockup sound booth
- [ ] Confirm start time either against Planning Center or Worship Leader
If there is a special service or an unusual amount of people on the stage, either an increase or decrease then the norm, discuss with the worship leader about changing the 8:30 AM arrival time, less people and well-known songs can push the arrival time to 9 AM, while an increase of people or a holiday/special guest service can pull it sooner to 8 or 7:45 AM.



## Sunday Pre-Service
Arrival time of the band is typically 8:30AM PST, try to arrive at or before that time depending on the number of performers.
- [ ] If you are early, before staff, then power on the sanctuary lights, power on the board and then the closet wireless inputs and the speakers 3rd.
if the 3 wireless are muted they do not have to be second, but the board must be turned on before the speakers or the boards power will pop out of the speakers, not damaging but loud and annoying.
- [ ] Pull your saved "Service" scene
- [ ] Wake/Power on the PC, if you are after staff most likely it is setup already with the PowerPoint for the day
- [ ] Bring out Camcorder and attach it to roof boom arm, plug in Boards audio outputs and PCs HDMI Input
- [ ] Once band is setup get a base level of audio of the needed instruments, some guitar, bass, all vocals, make sure there's no unexpected sounds or buzzing and everything is audible
- [ ] Setup base level for returns, if known, setup how that performer usually likes their in-ears
- [ ] Worship leader will start off with a prayer and then will start the first song, use that first song to go over any changes to the libraries for the band, get the levels setup for the instruments and then the vocalists
- [ ] First song may complete or could stop partially from not being able to hear, go over with everyone using in-ears for if they need more or less from every member
- [ ] First song will restart and should complete the 2nd time. After 2nd run confirm with everyone that everything is audible
- [ ] Proceed with other 4 songs, fine tuning peoples in-ears and room setup
