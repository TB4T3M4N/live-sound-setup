# Day-of-show runbook

A short flow you can follow when the room is already familiar.

1. **Arrive and walk the room.** Power, load-in path, and anything that moved since last time.
2. **Stage the gear.** Mixer in a place you can see the room. Cables routed safely.
3. **Power on and noise-check.** Listen before anyone is at a mic.
4. **Line check.** Every input, then a quick pass at a low show level.
5. **Wireless and playback.** Fresh batteries, test the first song or walk-in track.
6. **Hands off until start.** Leave the system in a known-good state.
7. **Show.** Watch the room, not only the meters. Ride spoken-word mics.
8. **Strike.** Power down safely, pack against the inventory, write one note about what to fix.

If something fails during the show, take a breath, mute the bad channel, and swap a cable or mic before you start changing a lot of settings.
